Overview for Tutor Only 
In this lesson, your student will learn that in the middle ofa three­syllable word, the vowel in an Open-I orOpen-E syllable will make its Short sound. 
The position ofthis syllable is important. This only happens in the middle syllable. Therefore, it will only happen ifa word has three or more syllables. 
The name 'Confident Rule' is a reminder that an Open-I syllable in the middle ofa word makes its Short sound. (Open-E syllables in the middle will make their Short-E sounds, but since those words are rare, they are treated as part ofthe Confident rule.) 
Technically, d1is is a variation ofthe Schwa rule. But to avoid confusion, the Barton System doesn't call it a Schwa. (We reserve the term Schwa for the /uh/ sound.) Sojustcall this the Confident 
• Rule. 
As you '11 see, words can follow both the Confident Rule andthe Banana Rule. 
Sprinkle these qllestions throughout the lesson after you've taught d1e new material: 
• 
Does that word use the Confident rule or Banana rule? 

• 
Why is that Open-I making a Short sound? 


SUMMARY: 
• The Confident rule: 
In the middle syllabic, Open-I's and Open-E's make their Short sounds. 
• 
To be in the middle syllable, a word has to have 3 syllables or more. 

• 
A word can follow both the Confident rule and the Banana rule.

When Spelling, Student Leaves Out a Sound 
This happens most often in words that contain three-letter blends, such as Constrain. 
After the student has spelled the word, tell him everything he has 
is correct, but one sound is missing. Then teach him how to find 
it, using this procedure: 
Get his eyes off ofthe word and back to your mouth. 
Say just the first syllable, have him repeat it at normal speed, then use the "swoop in the air" gesture to get him to say it slower. 
Then have him look at the first syllable while saying it slowly to see if every sound is there. • 
Then get his eyes back to your mouth, and repeat the process for the second syllabic (and the third) until the missing sound is found.
