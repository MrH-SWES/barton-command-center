Overview for Tutor Only 
Inthis lesson, your student willadd suffixes to Silent-E words. Inthis 
process, you 'II teach a new spelling rule: the Dropping rule. 
As before, ifa suffix starts with a consonant, you can just add it. But ifa suffix starts with a vowel, you must drop the Silent-E before adding the suffix. 
So Tape becomes Taping. As before, a vowel suffix acts like a magnet, pulling offthe last letter, so Taping divides as Ta-ping. You '11 also remind your student that the Baseword Tap needs the Doubling Rule, and becomes Tapping. 
But ifa Baseword ends in CE or GE, you would notdrop the Silent-E ifyou're adding a suffix that does not start with a Watch­Outvowel (such as ABLE) because that would change the sound of the C or G. So Replace becomes Replaceable, and Change becomes Changeable. 
Sprinkle these questions through the lesson: 
Why did(or didn't) youdrop the Silent-E? 
What is the Baseword? 
What kind ofletter do you drop?

The Dropping Rule: Drop a Silent-E when adding a Vowel suffix. (tape, taping) 

• 
Don't drop the Silent-E ifthe Baseword ends in CE or GE and the suffix does not start with a Watch-Out vowel. (replace, replaceable) 


• • Contrast the Dropping rule with the Doubling rule. (taping versus tapping)
