Overview for Tutor Only 
Inthis lesson, you'll teach two vowel teams that make the same 
sound: OI and OY. Since words can't end in I, OI is used at the 
beginning or in the middle ofwords; OY is used at the end. 
Then you'll introduce another vowel team that ends in Y: EY, which says lee/ as in Turkey. (In only four words, does EYsays /ail: They, Obey, Convey, and Survey. In those words, EY is accented. Those are treated as Sight Words.) 
This is nota spelling rule, as EY is notthe most common way to spell /eel at the end ofa word. Ifyour student is not sure how to spell /eel at the end, have him look it up using Y-which isthe most common spelling of/ee/ at the end. 
In New Teaching #2, you'll introduce three Prefixes that end with 
0: Co (with), Macro (big), and Micro (small). 
Ifa Prefix ends with 0, and the Baseword sta~s with I, it does not create an 01 vowel team. (Coinsure)

Scorpions 
According to a recent report, Arizona has thirty-six different kinds of scorpions. Scorpions prey on crickets, roaches, and grasshoppers. The small straw-colored bark scorpion is the only one likely to come into a house. Titis scrawny insect is able to crawl through the tiniest cracks because he is only about one-quarter inch long. He has four pairs of legs and claws on the front ofhis body. The stinger is on the end ofa long tail, which he curls and carries over top of his body. 
Last year in Arizona, six thousand scorpion stings were reported. No one in Arizona has died from a scorpion sting during the past forty years, but a sting is painful. 
It may cause you to feel nervous or restless. Check with your doctor right away. He may suggest that you wash the site with soap and water and put a cold compress on the bite.

The Under,:round Railroad 
Bob Brown resides in a rambling old mansion near the river. His house is on the Registry of Historic Places. It is famous because it had been a station on the Underground Railroad. The house seems quite mysterious because it's filled with hidden rooms and passageways. 
During the days when slavery was allowed in the South, many citizens hated the idea that anyone could be owned as property. They joined with others to encourage the slaves to escape. Some made speeches about the evils of slavery. Others became authors and published books that generated sympathy for the slaves. 
Frequently, desperate slaves attempted to flee from their owners. If they could make it to Canada, they would be free. But it was a long and dangerous trip. Many abolitionists helped by providing temporary safe haven in their homes. This system of homes became known as the Underground Railroad. 
Many of the slaves were exhausted and starving when they arrived at these homes in the pitch dark. They would have been incapable of surviving the long trip on foot to Canada without the protection of people like Bob Brown's ancestors.

The Depression 
The Depression hit when I was growing up. Times were tough. When the factory closed its doors, Dad was out of a job. Dad was a proud man. He thought the world was in an awful mess, and he denounced the government. 
Grandma said we shouldn't worry. She said, ''The Lord will provide.'' Dad said it was true that God gives every bird its food, but the Lord doesn't exactly throw it into its nest. 
Dad bounced around from job to job, but it was clear that if things didn't improve soon, we'd be joining the rest of the crowd in the soup line. 
I dropped out of school to help pay the rent and put food on the table. I was willing to do any kind of work that would bring in some money. But as the Depression grew worse, no jobs could be found in our town. 
Uncle Dan had a small house in the South. Dad thought we could manage better if we pooled everything we had and moved in with Uncle Dan. In those days, families stuck together and helped one another. 
After a new president was elected, people began to have hope, and things slowly changed for the better.

Captain ,Tames Cook 
In Marco Polo's day, a notion existed that there was a huge unknown continent in the South Pacific that counterbalanced the northern continents and kept the world from turning upside­down. Marco Polo had described a new land filled with gold, and some believed his utopia must be the unknown continent. If true, the nation that discovered it would be very rich. 
In 1768, an English explorer named Captain James Cook was secretly appointed by the King to search the South Pacific for this unknown continent. Captain Cook was a self-made man. He started his career as a grocer's apprentice, then worked for a coal shipper for nine years. But he had a craving for adventure, so he joined the British Navy. Captain Cook became known as an extremely good navigator. 
He made three voyages between 1768 and 1780, covering thousands ofmiles. He took scientists and artists on all ofhis voyages. He kept detailed, accurate observations from which he later wrote several books. His voyages produced more reliable information about the nature ofthe world than those ofany previous explorer. 
Captain Cook also took good care ofhis men. He experimented with various foods and found a way to keep his crew free ofscurvy during the long trips. 
But he did not discover any gold, and his voyages laid to rest the myth ofan imaginary continent.
